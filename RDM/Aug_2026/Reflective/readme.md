Use [GRADING_RUBRIC_AND_CALIBRATION.md](GRADING_RUBRIC_AND_CALIBRATION.md) for the marking protocol agreed from the tutor's instructions and supplied materials on 6 October 2026.

- 20 subcriteria scored independently as 0, 0.5 or 1, followed by cross-cohort calibration.
- University weights: knowledge 25%, application 25%, critical evaluation 25%, reading 15%, presentation 10%.
- Tutor-specified academic mean target: 68 after the EUT/Bayes eligibility cap and before word-count penalties; no forced normal distribution or assumed SD target.
- A demonstrated application of at least one of EUT or Bayes is required for eligibility for 70+. Otherwise the overall academic mark is capped at 69.
- Whole-number reporting with transparent weighted calculations, caps and rounding; ties take priority if exact integer mean preservation is impossible.
- Confirmed assessed word counts above 1,650 incur a separate 10 percentage-point deduction. No automatic underlength penalty.
