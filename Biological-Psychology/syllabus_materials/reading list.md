# Notes for Later Seminar and Assessment Integration

When seminar materials are reviewed, suitable anchor readings can be selected from this list using the same principle applied in Health Psychology:

**read → use in seminar → apply/evaluate in assessment**

Particular priorities are likely to include:

- **Units 1–3:** Henson (2005), Poldrack & Wagner (2004), Bandettini (2009), Chatterjee (2005), Fellows et al. (2005) for methodological reasoning and the CLQ;
- **Unit 5:** Gazzaniga (2005), Rees et al. (2002), Zeman (2001), plus Snyder (2019) / Sukhera (2022) for narrative-review methodology;
- **Unit 6:** H.M./Molaison and Eichenbaum for memory;
- **Unit 7:** Friederici (2011) and Price (2012) for language;
- **Unit 8:** LeDoux (2012) for emotion;
- **Unit 9:** contrasting biological and integrative accounts such as Banner (2013), Disner et al. (2011), Howes & Kapur (2009), Flint & Kendler (2014), and Fatemi & Folsom (2009).

The final narrative review should ultimately require students to move beyond textbook description and demonstrate substantive engagement with module scholarship plus independently sourced peer-reviewed research.
