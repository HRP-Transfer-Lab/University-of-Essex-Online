### Unit 5: Consciousness and narrative literature reviews

How does activity in the brain relate to our conscious experience? In Unit 5, we will explore concepts and theories of consciousness, considering what **blindsight, split-brain syndrome and hemispatial neglect** can tell us about awareness and brain function. Revisiting blindsight and neglect from Unit 4 will also help you connect our discussions of perception and attention with questions about consciousness.

This unit introduces **narrative literature reviews**, the approach you will use in your final assessment. The aim is to bring research together into a critical, organised discussion of a focused topic—not simply summarise studies one after another. You will begin developing your approach to searching the literature, integrating biological explanations with empirical findings, and identifying debates and gaps in our understanding.

### Unit 6: Learning and memory

How does an experience become a memory? In Unit 6, we will examine learning and memory at two interconnected levels: changes at the synapses between neurons and the organisation of memory systems across the brain.

We will explore **synaptic plasticity**—how neural connections strengthen or weaken with experience—and consider how this supports learning. We will also examine short-term and long-term memory, declarative and non-declarative memory, and working memory. Throughout, we will consider how brain imaging, animal studies and studies of people with amnesia contribute to our understanding.
