# Unit 2 Welcome Email

**Subject:** Welcome to Unit 2 – The Brain and Nervous System

Dear everyone,

Welcome to **Unit 2 of Biological Psychology!**

Having explored the foundations and history of biological psychology in Unit 1, we now turn to the biological machinery that makes thought, emotion and behaviour possible.

This week, we'll explore the **structure and function of neurones, how nerve cells communicate through electrical and chemical signalling, and how the brain and wider nervous system are organised**. These foundations will be important as we move on to examine the biological basis of psychological processes such as perception, memory, language and emotion.

As you work through the materials, try to connect what you're learning about neural structures and processes to their roles in behaviour. An important theme throughout the module is understanding not only how the nervous system works, but also how we can use scientific evidence to investigate relationships between brain and behaviour.

## Collaborative Learning Question (CLQ) – Opens Tomorrow!

Our first assessed activity, the **Collaborative Learning Question (CLQ), will open tomorrow, Tuesday 20 October 2026, at 9:00 am (UK time).**

For this assessment, you'll select an empirical study involving human participants that investigates a relationship between the nervous system and thought, emotion or behaviour. You'll prepare an initial **500-word contribution** and subsequently engage critically with **two classmates' contributions (300 words each)**.

As you begin thinking about possible studies, consider not only what the researchers discovered, but also **how they investigated the brain–behaviour relationship and what conclusions their methods allow them to draw**. We'll develop these critical evaluation skills further in Unit 3.

Please take some time to familiarise yourselves with the CLQ assessment guidance and begin considering a suitable study.

I hope you enjoy Unit 2, and I look forward to seeing your contributions to the discussion!
