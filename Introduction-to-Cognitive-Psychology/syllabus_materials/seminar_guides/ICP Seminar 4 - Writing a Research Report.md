## Aims

The seminar provides guidance on writing a complete research report based on an experiment from the module.

### This week we will

- Review the standard sections of a research report.
- Discuss how to integrate the literature review.
- Practise formulating clear hypotheses.
- Share strategies for interpreting results and discussing real-world applications.

## Test Your Knowledge

- The purpose and format of each section of a research report.
- Strategies for clearly stating hypotheses and interpreting results.
- Using literature to contextualise studies and discuss applications.
- APA formatting and academic-writing conventions.

## Social Learning

Give constructive feedback on peers' draft Research Report sections.

## Check Your Understanding

1. What makes a good hypothesis? What are some criteria for evaluating hypotheses?
2. What strategies can you use to interpret and contextualise your results based on prior literature?

## Discuss & Debate

1. What are some key ways of evaluating the strengths and limitations of a study?
2. In what ways might culture or individual differences influence the generalisability of a study's findings?
