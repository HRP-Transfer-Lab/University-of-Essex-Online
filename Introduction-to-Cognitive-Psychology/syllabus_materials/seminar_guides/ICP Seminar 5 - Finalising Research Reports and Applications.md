## Aims

The seminar focuses on finalising research reports, with emphasis on **real-world applications of cognitive psychology**.

### This week we will

- Review key criteria for the research-report assessment.
- Discuss strategies for the Discussion section and real-world applications.
- Learn about translational cognitive psychology and its applied value.

## Test Your Knowledge

- Key criteria for a high-quality research report.
- Connecting study findings to real-world contexts in the Discussion.
- Principles of translational cognitive psychology.
- Examples of applying cognitive-psychology theories and findings.

## Social Learning

Give constructive feedback to peers to improve research reports.

## Check Your Understanding

1. What makes for an insightful and relevant discussion of real-world applications in a research report?
2. How might principles of cognitive psychology be applied in your chosen career field or profession?

## Discuss & Debate

1. What are some important ethical considerations when translating cognitive research to applied settings?
2. What role can cognitive psychology play in overcoming societal challenges?
