## Aims

The seminar is designed to introduce students to the ethics of cognitive psychology research as students and professionals. Preparation should use information from the textbooks and readings.

### This week we will

- Provide an overview of the module.
- Learn the four core ethical principles outlined by the British Psychological Society:
  - Respect
  - Competence
  - Responsibility
  - Integrity
- Examine how these principles apply to research in cognitive psychology, cognitive neuroscience and neuropsychology.
- Discuss ethical considerations surrounding the use of generative AI such as ChatGPT in academic research and education.

## Test Your Knowledge

- The four core ethical principles from the BPS Code of Ethics.
- Examples of ethical issues that may arise in cognitive psychology research.
- Benefits and limitations of using generative AI tools such as ChatGPT.
- Guidelines for the ethical use of AI in academic work.

## Social Learning

Critically analyse the ethical implications of using AI tools such as ChatGPT in psychology research and education.

## Check Your Understanding

1. What are some key ethical considerations when conducting research with vulnerable populations such as children or older adults?
2. How might the use of generative AI tools affect goals and standards in psychology education if not used carefully?

## Debate

1. Is it ever ethically justifiable to use deception in psychological research?
2. Can AI tools such as ChatGPT be utilised in beneficial ways that align with ethical principles, or do the risks outweigh potential benefits?
