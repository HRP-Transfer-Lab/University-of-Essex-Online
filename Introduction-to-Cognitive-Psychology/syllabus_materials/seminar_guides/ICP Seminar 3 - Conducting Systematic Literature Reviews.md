## Aims

The seminar provides guidance on conducting systematic literature reviews using a **simplified version of PRISMA-P**.

### This week we will

- Learn about the purpose and process of systematic literature reviews.
- Understand the relevant flow diagram and sections of a PRISMA-P review for the assessment.
- Practise developing a focused review question and search strategy.

## Test Your Knowledge

- Developing a focused review question and search strategy.
- Key PRISMA-P components and content.
- Effective search strategies.
- Key databases and sources for psychology literature.

## Social Learning

Provide constructive feedback to peers on their planned review methods and topics.

## Check Your Understanding

1. What makes a research question suitable for a systematic literature review?
2. What challenges do you anticipate in screening studies and extracting data based on inclusion/exclusion criteria?

## Discuss & Debate

1. What are some key criteria for assessing the quality and risk of bias in studies included in a review?
2. In what ways can AI tools be used appropriately to assist with the literature-review process? What are the associated limitations or ethical issues?
