# Notes for Later Seminar and Assessment Integration

Potential high-value assessment anchors are already visible in the reading list:

- **CLQ / change blindness:** Rensink et al. (1997), Posner (2012), Milner & Goodale, Katsuki & Constantinidis (2014), Diamond (2013), plus the three approaches introduced in Unit 1.
- **Scientific Report — Misinformation Effect:** Loftus & Palmer (1974), Kaplan et al. (2016), relevant reconstructive-memory sources, and wider independently sourced literature.
- **Scientific Report — Emotional Stroop:** emotion-regulation and emotion-cognition sources from Unit 6, supplemented by experiment-specific journal literature.
- **Unit 7 formative literature review:** readings should be used to practise systematic searching, theoretical synthesis and real-world application rather than simple article summary.

The same design principle used in the other modules should be applied here:

**read → use in seminar → apply/evaluate in assessment**
