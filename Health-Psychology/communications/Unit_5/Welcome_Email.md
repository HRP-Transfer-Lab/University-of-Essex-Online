## This week's focus

This week, we turn our attention to **stress, its relationship with health, and how we cope**. Building on Unit 4's focus on individual differences, a useful question to keep in mind is: **why might two people facing similar stressful circumstances experience very different health outcomes?**

We'll explore different definitions of stress, the role of our perceptions and interpretations, and the physical, psychological and behavioural pathways that may link stress with health and illness. This includes **psychoneuroimmunology**—the study of relationships between psychological processes, the nervous system and immune functioning—as well as the ways stress may influence health-related behaviours.

We'll also examine theories of coping and consider how personality, coping style, social support and interventions can shape the management of stress. As you work through the unit materials and assigned reading, ask not only whether a coping strategy is helpful, but **for whom, in which circumstances, and why**.

Keep a critical perspective on the evidence: does a study show an association between stress and illness, or does its design support a stronger causal conclusion? How well does it account for other influences on health? The aim is to understand these relationships through a **biopsychosocial perspective**, rather than treating stress as a single, straightforward cause of illness.

**Please note that the seminar taking place this week will cover Unit 6 content:**

**Stress, Health, Coping and Adapting to Chronic Illness**

The session will connect our work on stress and coping with the experience of chronic illness, from diagnosis through to adjustment. Please prepare using the **core textbooks and recommended reading**, and look ahead to the relevant Unit 6 materials as well as working through Unit 5.

Come ready to discuss:

- **Individual differences:** Why do some people become ill in response to stress while others don't?

- **Psychological influences:** To what extent can psychological factors influence health and illness processes?
- **Barriers to adaptation:** What does research tell us about the factors that can make adjustment to chronic illness more difficult?
