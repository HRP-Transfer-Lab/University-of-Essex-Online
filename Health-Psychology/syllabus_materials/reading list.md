The module reading list labels this resource as **2020**. The Health 2020 policy framework itself was developed and adopted earlier; the official WHO materials below provide the framework and subsequent implementation material.

- [WHO Health 2020 policy framework PDF](https://iris.who.int/bitstream/handle/10665/326386/9789289002790-eng.pdf)
