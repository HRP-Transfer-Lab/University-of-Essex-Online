# Unit 4

## Description

The reading this week focuses on the topic of health inequalities and how this impacts the individual in terms of health outcomes. The reading in Morrison & Bennett's chapters will also outline the concept of 'quality of life', how it is measured, and the reasons why it is perceived differently by individuals. Katon (2011) reviews the bidirectional relationship between depression and chronic medical illness and the association of depression with problems in the physician-patient relationship, health risk behaviours, medical symptom burden, functional impairment, adherence to self-care regimens, medical complications, and mortality.
