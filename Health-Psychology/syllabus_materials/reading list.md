# Unit 2

## Description

The reading this week focuses on enhancing your understanding of health psychology theory. The chapters in the required reading list review social cognition models of health psychology and describe how they can be applied to health promotion. The additional readings are selected papers which critically review a range of social cognition models applied to health.

## Required Reading

### Straub, R. O. (2023). *Health Psychology: A Biopsychosocial Approach* (7th ed.)

For Unit 2, read **Part 3, Chapters 6 and 7**.

Focus on:

- **Staying Healthy: Primary Prevention and Positive Psychology**
- **Exercise, Sleep, and Injury Prevention**

> **Bibliographic note:** The module list gives the title **“Health promotion by social cognitive means”** with the year **1998** and journal **Health Education & Behavior**. The 1998 Bandura paper is actually *Health promotion from the perspective of social cognitive theory* in *Psychology & Health*. Bandura later published *Health Promotion by Social Cognitive Means* in *Health Education & Behavior* in 2004. The 2004 paper is linked below in case this is the source the module intended:
>
> - Bandura, A. (2004). Health promotion by social cognitive means. *Health Education & Behavior, 31*(2), 143–164. [https://doi.org/10.1177/1090198104263660](https://doi.org/10.1177/1090198104263660)
