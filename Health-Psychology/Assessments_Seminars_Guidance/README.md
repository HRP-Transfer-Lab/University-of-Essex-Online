# Assessments and Seminars Guidance

This folder contains assessment-specific marking guidance and seminar-support material for the Health Psychology module.

The guidance is designed to sit underneath the official University of Essex Online grading criteria. The official criteria, weightings and grade-band descriptors remain authoritative. The subcriteria here are diagnostic prompts intended to improve consistency of marking and feedback.

## Diagnostic scoring convention

For each subcriterion, use:

- **1** = fully and convincingly satisfied
- **0.5** = partially, unevenly or ambiguously satisfied
- **0** = absent, seriously incorrect or not meaningfully demonstrated

These diagnostic values are **not an arithmetic grading formula**. They help inform the holistic percentage awarded under each official criterion.

## Files

- `CLQ_Academic_Poster_Criteria_and_Subcriteria.md` — Unit 3–4 Collaborative Learning Question: theory-led health promotion poster, 500-word executive summary and two peer responses.
- `End_of_Module_Essay_Criteria_and_Subcriteria.md` — Unit 9 end-of-module chronic illness essay.
- `Seminar_Led_Reading_Integration.md` — core/supplementary readings for Seminars 1–5, suggested reading-led seminar activities, and assessment connections.
- `CLQ_Grading_Specification.md` — operational cohort grading specification for the CLQ.
- `End_of_Module_Essay_Grading_Specification.md` — operational cohort grading specification for the final essay.

## Source hierarchy

1. Official assessment-specific grading rubric.
2. Official assessment brief and submission instructions.
3. Module learning outcomes, reading list and seminar/formative guidance.
4. The diagnostic subcriteria in this folder.

Where there is any conflict, the official rubric and assessment brief take precedence.
